## Overview

This track teaches the *customer-embedded delivery craft* of AI engineering end-to-end for a hands-on specialist IC at level 35: the Forward Deployed Engineer role Palantir popularised and that OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale AI, Sierra, Decagon, Hebbia, Writer, Harvey, Glean, Cresta, Cursor/Anysphere, Replit, Vercel, Snowflake, Databricks, and the hyperscaler customer-engineering orgs now hire for under the same or closely-equivalent titles. The modules step from the role's discipline and positioning → customer discovery and pilot scoping → enterprise integration against messy customer stacks (SSO, data APIs, SaaS platforms, legacy systems, networking, MCP) → LLM application engineering with the customer-environment overlay (provider selection, gateway routing, per-tenant model routing, cost/latency SLOs) → retrieval and grounding on customer data with ACL propagation and PII-safe retrieval → agentic workflows on enterprise surfaces with OWASP Agentic hardening → eval engineering on customer data with SME-paired gold sets and business-KPI linkage → enterprise security, privacy, and compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, FedRAMP, GDPR, OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001) → deployment, rollout, and change management with feature flags and canary/shadow/kill-switch rigor → observability and shared on-call for customer-production systems → the field→product feedback loop → consulting craft and executive communication.

It is a **hands-on specialist IC track at level 35**, positioned in the **AI Engineering family**, between [`applied-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30, in-house applied LLM feature engineering) and [`senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning) / [`ai-engineering-manager-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-engineering-manager-learning) (level 40, build leadership and engineering management). The differentiator against peer level-30 tracks ([`applied-ai-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning), [`agentic-ai-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning), [`ai-eval-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning)) and against peer level-35 tracks ([`ai-infra-inference-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-inference-engineer-learning), [`ai-infra-security`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning), [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning), [`training-pipeline-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/training-pipeline-engineer-learning), [`voice-agent-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/voice-agent-engineer-learning), [`ai-enablement-lead`](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-enablement-lead-learning)) is *customer-embedded delivery ownership*: this curriculum builds the pilot inside a customer's environment, integrates against their stack, carries the security and compliance review, runs shared on-call with their operators, closes the field→product loop, and does the consulting-craft work that applied-AI engineering in-house does not require.

Total planned commitment: **170 hours across 12 modules** + **108 hours across 4 projects** = **~278 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the customer-embedded delivery craft end-to-end — Forward Deployed Engineer discipline and positioning, customer discovery and pilot scoping, enterprise integration engineering, LLM application engineering in customer environments (with the overlay on top of applied-ai-engineer's craft), grounding on customer data (with access-control propagation on top of rag-engineer's craft), agentic workflows on enterprise surfaces, eval engineering on customer data, enterprise security + privacy + compliance for AI deployments, deployment + rollout + change management with the customer, observability + on-call for shared customer-production systems, field→product feedback loop, and consulting craft + executive communication.
- **Defers down** to [`applied-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30, AI Engineering family) for in-house applied-AI feature engineering craft (prompts, structured outputs, caching, streaming, cost/latency SLOs); to [`agentic-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30, AI Engineering family) for deep agent-framework internals (LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK, MCP, tool-calling patterns); to [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer evaluation methodology depth; to [`rag-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning) (level 25, ML Engineering family) for retrieval-engine depth; to [`llm-application-developer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/llm-application-developer-learning) (level 25, AI Engineering family) for LLM app fundamentals; to [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20, ML Engineering family) for classical ML fundamentals; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10, Infra family) for engineering-craft prerequisites; to [`ai-infra-mlops-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation; to [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25, Governance family) for risk-engineering craft; to [`ai-governance-analyst-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15, Governance family) for operational analyst work.
- **Defers sideways** to [`ai-infra-inference-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-inference-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35, Infra family) for inference serving depth (vLLM / TGI / SGLang internals, batching, quantisation, KV-cache, autoscaling); to [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) / [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) (peer, level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale; to [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing packet composition; to [`training-pipeline-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/training-pipeline-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35, ML Engineering family) for training / fine-tune pipeline depth; to [`voice-agent-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/voice-agent-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35) for voice-channel specifics; to [`ai-enablement-lead-learning`](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-enablement-lead-learning) (peer, level 35) for change-management counterpart work; to [`ai-data-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ai-data-engineer-learning) (peer) for customer-side data pipeline work.
- **Defers up** to [`senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 40) for build leadership and shared-infra authorship; to [`ai-engineering-manager-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-engineering-manager-learning) (level 40) for engineering management scope; to [`agentic-systems-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-systems-architect-learning) (level 48) for multi-agent architecture; to [`principal-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/principal-ml-engineer-learning) (level 48) for principal IC scope; to [`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology depth; to [`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) and [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for program leadership / strategy / executive scope.
- **Out of scope** to legal counsel (customer contract review shapes requirements but no legal opinion is delivered); to sales (deal-level selling is adjacent, not owned); to full-time professional services delivery (the FDE is design-partner engineering, not billable consultancy); to customer-side change-management at organisational scale (coordinated with the customer's own operators and with `ai-enablement-lead` internally).

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — The Forward Deployed Engineer Discipline and Position on the Ladder
Characterise the FDE role as it has evolved from the Palantir origin to the modern frontier-lab (OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale AI) and applied-AI product company (Sierra, Decagon, Hebbia, Writer, Harvey, Glean, Cresta) instantiations — the embedded delivery stance, the no-ego engineering craft, the design-partner-first mindset, and the field→product loop as a first-class deliverable. Position the role on the level ladder against `applied-ai-engineer` (level 30), `ai-eval-engineer` (level 30), `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35), `ai-infra-inference-engineer` (level 35), `senior-agentic-ai-engineer` (level 40), and `ai-engineering-manager` (level 40). Diagram the relationship to adjacent customer-facing roles (Solutions Engineer, Pre-Sales Engineer, Customer Success Engineer, Professional Services Consultant, Partner Engineer, Developer Advocate) and enforce the sharp boundaries this track keeps. Decide the technical-generalist stance — when the FDE owns full stack end-to-end vs. when the FDE escalates to a peer specialist.

### mod-102 — Customer Discovery, Pilot Scoping, and Success Criteria
Run SME-paired customer discovery — Teresa Torres continuous-discovery interviewing, Jeff Patton story mapping, workflow process-mining, observe-first-code-later discipline. Decide where AI wins vs. where AI breaks — the 'can-a-human-do-this-in-a-minute' sanity check, the deterministic-code-beats-LLM test, the 'cost-per-task vs. value-per-task' viability check, the 'failure-mode is tolerable at pilot error-rate' safety check. Draft a pilot-scope document — problem statement, user stories, non-goals, success metrics, decision-record for stack choices, exit-to-production criteria, pre-mortem. Shape design-partner commercials with Product / Sales / Legal. Coordinate with `ai-enablement-lead` and `ai-risk-engineer` on the customer-side change-management and risk-engineering slices.

### mod-103 — Enterprise Integration Engineering
Wire the pilot into the customer's identity provider (SAML 2.0, OIDC 1.0, SCIM 2.0) against the big-4 IdPs (Okta, Entra, Ping, Google Workspace). Author service-to-service auth (OAuth 2.0 client-credentials, mTLS, token exchange). Integrate with the customer's data surface (REST / GraphQL / webhooks / CDC / event buses) and SaaS platforms (Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Workday, Snowflake, Databricks, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365). Handle the legacy surface (SOAP, SFTP, mainframe gateways) with explicit cost / reliability / latency trade-offs. Design the customer-side networking posture (PrivateLink, VPC peering, VPN, egress proxying). Expose a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server when the integration shape calls for it. Cite the boundary to `ai-infra-inference-engineer` on model-server deployment inside customer VPC and to `ai-data-engineer` on customer-side ETL.

### mod-104 — LLM Application Engineering in Customer Environments
Select a provider stack per customer deployment — frontier labs vs. hyperscaler managed vs. self-hosted — scoring against data-residency, compliance, latency, cost, and model-choice-freedom constraints. Deploy a model gateway (LiteLLM / Portkey / OpenRouter) inside the customer environment with per-tenant budget enforcement, failover, caching, PII guards, audit logs. Build production prompt architecture at customer level — structured outputs, streaming, prompt caching, prompt versioning + review, per-tenant prompt overrides. Author per-customer-contracted cost / latency SLOs. Decide when to escalate to fine-tune / adapter workflows and how to split responsibilities with `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, level 30) and `training-pipeline-engineer` (peer, level 35). Deep in-house applied-AI feature-engineering craft is linked to `applied-ai-engineer` (level 30).

### mod-105 — Grounding on Customer Data — Retrieval, Access, and Provenance
Select and wire a retrieval engine per customer deployment (Pinecone / Weaviate / Qdrant / Chroma / pgvector / Elasticsearch / Vespa / Azure AI Search / Vertex AI Search) against deployment-model, hybrid-search, filter-capability, cost, and compliance-posture scoring. Propagate the customer's permission model into the retriever — document ACLs, row-level security, group / role / attribute filters — with explicit invalidation for permission changes. Ship PII-safe retrieval (Presidio or equivalent at ingest + retrieval + output boundaries, token-level redaction, reversible pseudonymisation when the business flow requires it). Design the citation / evidence / freshness UX. Bootstrap a customer-data gold set for retrieval eval. Deep retrieval-engine depth is linked to `rag-engineer` (level 25).

### mod-106 — Agentic Workflows on Enterprise Surfaces
Choose the right agent shape from Anthropic's 'Building effective agents' pattern catalogue (prompt chaining, routing, parallelisation, orchestrator-workers, evaluator-optimiser, autonomous agent) — with the deterministic-code-beats-LLM reflex for the parts that do not need an LLM. Implement production agents with Claude Agent SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK, LangGraph, or an in-house orchestrator. Design the human-in-the-loop checkpoint model per action class. Map agent tools to the customer's permission model with least-privilege per-tool-call, per-session scoping, per-tenant tool allow-lists, and an audit trail the customer's security team will subpoena. Harden against OWASP Agentic Top 10 (indirect injection, tool-abuse, memory poisoning, confused deputy, non-human identity, authorisation drift). Deep agent-framework depth is linked to `agentic-ai-engineer` (level 30); frontier-agent red-team depth is linked to `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40).

### mod-107 — Eval Engineering on Customer Data
Bootstrap a customer-data gold set — SME-paired labelling workflow, stratified sampling, double-labelling + inter-rater reliability, labelling-UI shape, versioned storage. Design the offline eval (task correctness, judge-as-LLM, pairwise comparison, factuality / groundedness / citation-accuracy) with eval-vs-metric-vs-business-KPI mapping. Ship the eval tooling stack (Promptfoo + RAGAS + DeepEval + Inspect) at the customer's compliance posture, wired into pilot CI/CD so a PR cannot merge until the eval gate passes. Ship online eval on production customer traffic with judge-as-LLM at sample rate, implicit user-feedback capture, per-tenant quality dashboards, drift detection. Run the pilot-to-production exit eval on a holdout slice and author the decision document the customer's governance board signs. Keep customer data private (eval infrastructure in VPC or zero-retention sandbox). Methodology depth is linked to `ai-eval-engineer` (level 30); release-assurance methodology is linked to `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35).

### mod-108 — Enterprise Security, Privacy, and Compliance in Customer Environments
Pass the customer's security review (SOC 2 Type II evidence collection, SIG / CAIQ responses, pen-test report sharing, data-flow diagrams, sub-processor disclosures) at onboarding pace. Meet vertical-specific regulatory asks (HIPAA + BAA for health, FedRAMP Moderate / High for federal, PCI-DSS for payments, EU AI Act Articles 9-15 + 72 for EU high-risk, GDPR 22 + 32 + 35, CCPA, state-level LL144 / Colorado AI Act / NYC bias audits). Threat-model the pilot per OWASP LLM Top 10 + Agentic Top 10 + MITRE ATLAS and ship mitigations before go-live. Negotiate the data contract (zero-retention, data-residency, BYOK, DPA shape, audit-log retention). Package AI supply-chain provenance (model / system / dataset cards, CycloneDX ML-BOM, SPDX AI, Sigstore / SLSA attestations, ModelScan + picklescan). Deep MLSec at platform scale is linked to `ai-infra-security` / `security-learning` (level 35); regulator-facing packet composition is linked to `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35); risk-engineering craft is linked to `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25).

### mod-109 — Deployment, Rollout, and Change Management with the Customer
Deploy inside the customer's environment — SaaS, dedicated SaaS, VPC install (Terraform / Helm shipped by the FDE), on-prem for air-gapped customers — with explicit per-environment trade-offs on latency / model choice / update cadence / support model. Ship a feature-flag strategy with LaunchDarkly / Statsig / Unleash — per-tenant, per-user, per-prompt, per-model rollout, shadow deployments, kill-switch controls, audit-log of every flag flip. Run canary + shadow + A/B rollouts on LLM features with explicit success criteria and blameless rollback. Version per-tenant artifacts (prompts, retrievers, models, tool allow-lists, judges) with explicit lineage and a replay workflow. Hand off to the customer-side operator with admin dashboards, SME training materials, and playbooks for common failure modes. Author the pilot-to-production hand-off packet.

### mod-110 — Observability and On-Call for Shared Customer Systems
Instrument the pilot with OpenTelemetry (traces, metrics, logs) using GenAI semantic conventions so customer-side SIEM / observability (Datadog, Splunk, Elastic, Grafana, Dynatrace, New Relic) consume them natively. Wire the LLM-observability platform (Langfuse / LangSmith / Arize Phoenix / Weave / Braintrust) at the customer's compliance posture. Author LLM-specific SLOs (quality, latency TTFT / TPOT / p50-p95-p99, cost per task / tenant / day, safety refusal rate / guardrail trip rate) tied to the customer's business KPI. Ship the on-call rota for a shared customer-production system with joint incident command, LLM-specific failure-mode triage (prompt regression, provider outage, retrieval drift, hallucination spike, cost blowout, guardrail false-positive storm), SEV classification, customer-visible status, and blameless post-mortems with permanent regression-fixture back-feed. Design the field-feedback-into-eval loop so every incident becomes a regression fixture and every bug report becomes a gold-set entry.

### mod-111 — Field → Product Feedback Loop and Internal Advocacy
Capture field telemetry with explicit triage (bug = fix in pilot, feature = surface to product, platform gap = surface to platform engineering / senior-agentic-ai-engineer / ai-engineering-manager). Author design-partner artifacts (pattern writeups, pain-point taxonomies, 'what we learned' one-pagers, customer success stories at product-actionable depth) with a cadence product leadership can count on. Package platform requests with evidence-from-two-customers, proposed interface sketch, risk/scope size, and the workaround the FDE is shipping in the interim. Influence the roadmap without owning it — show up prepared, bring data, bring the customer voice, propose not demand. Build the FDE-team knowledge base (pattern catalogue, failure-mode library, deployment playbook, security-review-answer library). Coordinate with `ai-engineering-manager` on framing, `senior-agentic-ai-engineer` on shared-infra proposals, `ai-enablement-lead` on customer-education content.

### mod-112 — Consulting Craft — Executive Communication and Iteration Rhythm
Engineer demos for executive audiences (before/after narrative structure, SME third-party-credibility hook, staging + failure-recovery craft, no-fake-demo rule). Author the exec brief — one-pager a VP reads before the meeting, five-minute standup narrative, before/after KPI table. Run the weekly / fortnightly pilot review with the customer — standing agenda, status (green / yellow / red with rationale), decisions required, open risks, metric updates. Handle customer-side resistance (SME preferring legacy workflow, department viewing pilot as threat, adversarial IT review) with radical-transparency + pragmatic-respect stance. Scope with discipline — say no, say 'later', say 'the applied-ai-engineer in your org can own this after hand-off'; pre-empt scope creep with a decision log the customer countersigns. Knowledge-transfer to the customer's engineering team — pair-programming cadence, documentation, runbook walkthroughs, on-call shadowing — so the FDE becomes unnecessary at hand-off. Position against adjacent customer-facing roles honestly (not AE, not SE/pre-sales, not CSM, not implementation consultant).

## Project summaries

### project-101 — End-to-End Pilot for One Customer Workflow (36h)
Take one real customer workflow (or a realistic simulated customer archetype) from discovery to a working pilot inside a customer-shape environment. Deliver discovery brief, pilot-scope document with success criteria, integration design against the customer's identity / data / SaaS surface, LLM-feature implementation with provider + gateway + prompts + structured outputs + SLOs, grounding slice with ACL-propagated retrieval, agentic workflow if the pilot shape calls for it, eval bundle with customer-data gold set, security review packet, deployment runbook with feature flags + canary + hand-off, observability bundle, and executive-debrief packet. The pilot must be reproducible — all artifacts in a single bundle another FDE could pick up.

### project-102 — Enterprise Integration & Compliance Kit for One Target Vertical (28h)
Build a reusable kit (code + docs + templates) for one target vertical — health (HIPAA + BAA + de-id), federal (FedRAMP Moderate), EU financial services (EU AI Act + GDPR + DORA), or regulated high-tech (SOC 2 Type II + CCPA). Identity integration (SAML/OIDC/SCIM) against the big-4 IdPs, service auth (OAuth2 + mTLS + token exchange), one SaaS-platform connector, audit-log pipeline, security-review packet template, DPA / zero-retention / BYOK template, MCP server implementation for one representative enterprise tool surface. The kit must shorten the next customer deployment in this vertical to days-not-weeks for integration.

### project-103 — Eval + Observability Bundle on Customer-Shape Data (24h)
Ship an eval + observability bundle on a customer-shape corpus (synthetic customer or sanitised real). Customer gold set bootstrapped with SME-paired labelling, offline eval suite with Promptfoo + RAGAS + DeepEval + Inspect wired to eval-gated CI, online-eval infrastructure with judge-as-LLM + implicit feedback + drift detection, observability dashboards per LLM-specific SLO, blameless post-mortem for one injected failure mode with regression-fixture back-feed. The bundle must prove the pilot is ready-or-not-ready to go live and support the exit decision.

### project-104 — Field → Product Playbook & Executive Debrief Packet (20h)
Author the field → product playbook that lets the FDE team scale. Pattern writeup for one design-partner engagement, pain-point taxonomy from two customer archetypes, platform-request packet for the core team with evidence-from-two-customers, roadmap-influence deck for the product-review meeting, executive-debrief packet for the sponsoring customer VP, knowledge-transfer plan for the customer's engineering team, FDE-team knowledge-base slice. The playbook must be reusable by a next FDE on a next engagement.
