## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for a Forward Deployed AI Engineer (level 35, AI Engineering family) from the public references catalogued in `.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`applied-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30) for in-house applied LLM feature engineering craft (prompts, structured outputs, caching, streaming, cost/latency SLOs) — this track adds the customer-environment overlay, not the full craft.
   - *Down* to [`agentic-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30) for deep agent-framework internals (LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK, MCP, tool-calling patterns).
   - *Down* to [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (level 30) for application-layer evaluation methodology depth (traces, judges, RAG eval, eval-gated CI/CD, online eval, cost/latency/quality).
   - *Down* to [`rag-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning) (level 25) for retrieval-engine depth; to [`llm-application-developer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/llm-application-developer-learning) (level 25) for LLM app fundamentals; to [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical ML fundamentals; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-infra-inference-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-inference-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35, Infra family) for inference serving depth (vLLM / TGI / SGLang internals, batching, quantisation, KV-cache, autoscaling).
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) / [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) (peer, level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing packet composition.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (peer-down, level 25, Governance family) for risk-engineering craft (harm models, red-team, adversarial-ML, fairness, privacy, guardrails, risk register) the FDE consumes from governance partners.
   - *Up* to [`senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 40) for build leadership and shared-infra authorship; to [`ai-engineering-manager-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-engineering-manager-learning) (level 40) for engineering management scope; to [`agentic-systems-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-systems-architect-learning) (level 48) for multi-agent architecture; to [`principal-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/principal-ml-engineer-learning) (level 48) for principal IC scope; to [`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology depth; to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for executive scope.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel (customer contract review shapes requirements but no legal opinion is delivered); to sales (deal-level selling is adjacent, not owned); to full-time professional services delivery (the FDE is design-partner engineering, not billable consultancy).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings with `<!-- needs-research -->` so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty.
