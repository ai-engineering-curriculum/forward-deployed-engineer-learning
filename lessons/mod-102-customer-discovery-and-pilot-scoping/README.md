# mod-102-customer-discovery-and-pilot-scoping: Customer Discovery, Pilot Scoping, and Success Criteria

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Run SME-paired customer discovery — jobs-to-be-done interviewing (Teresa Torres continuous-discovery pattern), story-mapping (Jeff Patton), workflow process-mining, observe-first-code-later discipline — and translate findings into a candidate automation surface
- Decide where AI wins vs. where AI breaks — the 'can-a-human-do-this-in-under-a-minute' sanity check, the deterministic-code-beats-LLM test, the 'cost-per-task vs. value-per-task' viability check, the 'failure-mode is tolerable at pilot error-rate' safety check
- Draft a pilot-scope document — problem statement, user stories, non-goals, success metrics (business-KPI linked + model-quality linked), decision-record for stack choices, exit-to-production criteria, and a pre-mortem for the most likely failure modes
- Shape design-partner commercials with Product / Sales / Legal — what the customer is on the hook for (SME time, data access, pilot evaluation, governance review), what the FDE is on the hook for (discovery, build, iterate, hand-off), and the explicit non-asks
- Coordinate with `ai-enablement-lead` (peer, level 35) on customer-side change-management scoping; coordinate with `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) on the risk-engineering slice the customer's governance team will require before go-live
