# mod-111-field-to-product-loop: Field → Product Feedback Loop and Internal Advocacy

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours

## Learning objectives

- Capture field telemetry with the explicit triage of bug (ship a fix inside the pilot) vs. feature (surface to product for the next release) vs. platform gap (surface to platform-engineering / senior-agentic-ai-engineer / ai-engineering-manager at level 40)
- Author design-partner artifacts — pattern writeups, pain-point taxonomies, 'what we learned' one-pagers, customer success stories at a technical depth product can act on — with a cadence product leadership can count on
- Package platform requests for the core team — clear ask, evidence (at least two customer instances exhibiting the gap), proposed interface sketch, risk / scope size, and the alternative workaround the FDE is shipping in the interim
- Influence the roadmap without owning it — show up prepared to the product-review meeting, bring data, bring the customer voice, propose, do not demand; the FDE's roadmap influence rests on the credibility of the field pattern evidence
- Build the internal knowledge base — pattern catalogue, failure-mode library, deployment playbook, security-review-answer library — that scales the FDE team
- Coordinate with `ai-engineering-manager` (level 40) on roadmap framing; coordinate with `senior-agentic-ai-engineer` (level 40) on shared-infra proposals; coordinate with `ai-enablement-lead` (peer, level 35) on customer-education content
